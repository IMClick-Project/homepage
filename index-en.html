About me

My name is Mariola. I hold a Master's degree in Chemical Engineering and I am currently a PhD student interested in modeling, simulation, optimization and process control. I enjoy combining programming, science and engineering to understand complex systems and develop tools that help explain and solve problems.

From a young age I participated in math and informatics olympiads. These experiences strongly shaped the way I learn and think. They helped me discover the value of reasoning, creativity in problem solving, and discipline in learning, skills that have influenced my academic and professional path ever since.

This project was born from those experiences. My goal is to share knowledge in math, informatics and chemical engineering so that more students can discover their interest in science and technology, develop curiosity and find inspiration to keep learning.
